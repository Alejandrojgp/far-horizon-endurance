Fifteen years of running. Nearly a decade of ultras. UESCA ultrarunning coach. I still train. That is part of why the practice stays small. The most expensive lesson I own is a DNF on terrain I had not earned. I do not publish the method that grew from that. I use it.Quince años corriendo. Casi una década de ultras. Coach UESCA.
